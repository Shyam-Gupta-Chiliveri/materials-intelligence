SEM Fracture Classifier

Upload a scanning electron microscope image of a fracture surface. The model segments ductile dimples versus brittle cleavages and reports the area split.

Open live app View source on GitHub
  • U-Net with ResNet50 encoder
  • Pixel-wise ductile / brittle map
  • Trained on SEM fracture images of 4Cr13 axle steel
